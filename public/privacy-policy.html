Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 25, 2026 · Last updated: August 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FinTrackPro (“the app,” “we,” “us”) collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use the FinTrackPro personal finance application and its associated web app and backend service. By creating an account or using the app, you agree to the practices described here.

Who we are

FinTrackPro is developed and operated by Vincent J. Maro, an independent developer. If you have any questions about this policy or your data, contact us at vincentdit@gmail.com.

Information we collect

We collect only the information needed to provide the app’s features:

  • Account information — the email address and name you provide when you register, and a password. Your password is never stored in plain text; it is protected using a one-way cryptographic hash (bcrypt).
  • Financial information you enter — the accounts, balances, transactions, budgets, goals, and categories you create or import within the app.
  • App settings — your preferences, such as base currency, language, and whether biometric unlock is enabled.
  • Technical data — basic information required to operate the service securely, such as authentication tokens and timestamps used to synchronize your data across your devices.

We do not collect advertising identifiers, and the app does not include third-party advertising or analytics tracking.

Bank connections (Plaid)

If you choose to link a bank account, FinTrackPro uses Plaid to establish that connection. When this feature is enabled, you enter your bank credentials directly with Plaid — FinTrackPro does not see or store your bank login credentials. Plaid provides the app with transaction and balance data for the accounts you connect. Plaid’s handling of your information is governed by Plaid’s own privacy policy at plaid.com/legal. The current version of the app operates Plaid in a sandbox/mock mode and does not connect to real financial institutions unless and until this is explicitly enabled.

Exchange rates

To convert between currencies, the app retrieves publicly available exchange-rate data from a third-party rates provider. These requests contain no personal or financial information about you — only a request for current rates.

How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and authenticate you.
  • To store your financial data and synchronize it across the devices where you are signed in.
  • To provide the app’s core features: tracking expenses and income, budgets, goals, reports, and multi-currency conversion.
  • To maintain the security and reliability of the service.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Where your data is stored

Your account and financial data are stored on our backend service, which is hosted on Render (application hosting) and Neon (managed PostgreSQL database). These providers act as data processors on our behalf and may process data in the regions where their infrastructure operates. All communication between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.

On-device data and biometrics

Some data is kept on your device for the app to function, including a local copy of your data and your authentication tokens, which are held in your device’s secure storage. If you enable biometric unlock (Face ID or fingerprint), that authentication is performed entirely by your device’s operating system. Your biometric data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to or stored by us.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account exists. You can permanently delete your account at any time from within the app under Accounts → Settings → Delete account. Deleting your account immediately and permanently removes your account and all associated financial data from our servers, including any synced records and active sessions. This action cannot be undone. You may also email vincentdit@gmail.com to request deletion.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including encrypted transmission (HTTPS), hashed passwords, short-lived access tokens with rotating refresh tokens, and rate limiting on our authentication endpoints. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children’s privacy

FinTrackPro is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You can exercise most of these directly in the app (your data is visible and editable, and you can delete your account), or by contacting us at vincentdit@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, provide notice within the app. Your continued use of the app after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
